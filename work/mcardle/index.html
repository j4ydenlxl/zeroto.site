How Michelle Works

Two journeys. One agent.

For Buyers

Your search, structured.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A 30-minute conversation to understand neighborhood, school district, must-haves, and timeline.

  2. 02

    Curated showings

    You see only homes worth seeing. No 80-listing slogs through MLS.

  3. 03

    Offer strategy

    When the right one shows up, Michelle structures an offer the seller respects without overpaying.

  4. 04

    Closing coordination

    Inspections, attorney, mortgage, title. You sign the papers. She does the rest.

For Sellers

Your home, sold for more.

  1. 01

    Walk-through valuation

    Michelle visits, walks the property, and tells you what it is honestly worth and what it could be worth.

  2. 02

    Prep and stage

    Photography, staging consultation, and the small fixes that pay back ten to one.

  3. 03

    Strategic launch

    Listed at the right number, marketed across Douglas Elliman's network, brokers' open within the first week.

  4. 04

    Negotiate and close

    Multiple offers when possible. The right offer when it matters. Closed on your timeline.
