The approach

Patient with buyers. Honest with sellers.

The North Shore is a market of small differences. A house two blocks south of the Estates section is a different home than one two blocks north, even when the square footage matches. Buyers feel that. Sellers usually do not, until the offers come in.

Nancy spends real time in every home before it lists. Where the morning light falls. Which wall the buyers will see first. What needs to be quietly fixed and what is fine to leave alone. Those small calls add up to a number on the closing statement.

Twenty eight closings last year. One hundred and twenty six total in Garden City alone. Most of them came from a referral, which is the only kind of marketing she really trusts.
