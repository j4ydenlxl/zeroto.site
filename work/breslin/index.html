A note from Liz

The market whispers. You just have to listen carefully.

Real estate in Garden City is not a transaction. It is a quiet conversation between sellers who know what their home is worth and buyers who know exactly what they want. The job is to bring those two people together at the right moment.

I have spent years learning the rhythm of these streets. When a Wellington Road colonial should go on the market. When a Stewart Manor cape will sit for sixty days, and when it will sell in a weekend. When a buyer says "I love it" but really means "I want to keep looking."

What I bring is patience, preparation, and the kind of direct honesty my clients keep coming back for. If your timing is wrong, I will tell you. If your list price is wrong, I will tell you. If the offer on the table is the right one, I will tell you that, too.

"Strategic, prepared, and straightforward. That is the whole thing."
